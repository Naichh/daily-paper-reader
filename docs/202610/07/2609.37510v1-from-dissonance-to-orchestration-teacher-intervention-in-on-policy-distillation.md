## 摘要
同策略蒸馏（OPD）利用更强教师的反馈，在学生自身的推理轨迹上训练学生。教师干预可以改进这些轨迹，但也会改变学生学习的分布。我们的受控研究表明，仅凭 rollout 质量不足以作为分配教师指导的标准。更深的干预在 rollout 准确率上收益递减，同时增加离策略负荷。在受限 rollout 时域的训练探针中，学生准确率峰值和性能保持分别偏好不同的干预强度。偏好的干预深度和位置也因基准而异。这些发现促使我们提出 MAESTRO，它利用局部策略分歧来联合自适应教师何时接管以及生成多长时间。其{策略分歧分数}将教师加权的候选覆盖度与局部分布相似度相结合，并在推理段落内聚合。在八个数学推理基准上，对于 0.6B 和 1.7B Qwen3 学生，MAESTRO 在比较方法中均取得最高的宏平均准确率，其中 1.7B 学生在每个基准上均领先。相对于标准 OPD，MAESTRO 还将平均训练响应长度减少了 67.3%。代码见 https://github.com/yhao-wang/MAESTRO。

## Abstract
On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student on its own reasoning trajectories using feedback from a stronger teacher. Teacher interventions can improve these trajectories, but also change the distribution on which the student learns. Our controlled studies show that rollout quality alone is an incomplete criterion for allocating teacher guidance. Deeper intervention yields diminishing gains in rollout accuracy while increasing off-policy load. In a training probe with a restricted rollout horizon, peak student accuracy and performance retention favor different intervention strengths. The preferred intervention depth and placement also vary across benchmarks. These findings motivate MAESTRO, which uses local policy disagreement to jointly adapt when the teacher takes over and how long it generates. Its {policy disagreement score} combines teacher-weighted candidate coverage with local distribution similarity and is aggregated within reasoning paragraphs. Across eight mathematical reasoning benchmarks, MAESTRO achieves the highest macro-average accuracy among the compared methods for both 0.6B and 1.7B Qwen3 students, with the 1.7B student leading on every benchmark. MAESTRO also reduces average training response length by 67.3\% relative to standard OPD. The code is available at https://github.com/yhao-wang/MAESTRO.
